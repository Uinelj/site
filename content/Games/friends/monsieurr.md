## 🕹️ SNES

> [!tip] 🥇 [[Shadowrun]] ✨
> ![|110](https://images.igdb.com/igdb/image/upload/t_cover_big/co2yws.jpg) `🏷 CRPG` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Shadowrun)
> A cult cyberpunk CRPG built on the tabletop system — real character builds, real choices, real "wander the city and figure it out" energy. If Elder Scrolls IV/V hit, this is the 16-bit ancestor.

> [!tip] 🥈 [[Ogre Battle: The March of the Black Queen]] ✨
> ![|110](https://images.igdb.com/igdb/image/upload/t_cover_big/co634z.jpg) `🏷 Tactical RPG` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Ogre%20Battle%3A%20The%20March%20of%20the%20Black%20Queen)
> A real-time tactics RPG about a rebellion against an empire, with a genuinely reactive alignment system. Strategic depth without the usual turn-based grid tedium.

> [!tip] 🥉 [[Romancing SaGa 2]] ✨
> ![|110](https://images.igdb.com/igdb/image/upload/t_cover_big/co24oa.jpg) `🏷 JRPG` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Romancing%20SaGa%202)
> Non-linear, systems-driven JRPG spanning generations of emperors — the anti-Chrono Trigger in structure but with the same "genre classic" pedigree.

> [!quote] #4 [[The 7th Saga]] ✨
> ![|110](https://images.igdb.com/igdb/image/upload/t_cover_big/co8kzr.jpg) `🏷 JRPG` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/The%207th%20Saga)
> A brutal, cult-favorite JRPG where you pick one of seven heroes and the other six become AI rivals racing you to the end. Genuinely unique structure.

> [!quote] #5 [[Populous]] ✨
> ![|110](https://images.igdb.com/igdb/image/upload/t_cover_big/co2jjf.jpg) `🏷 God-Game · Strategy` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Populous)
> The genre-founding god-game — raise land, grow followers, smite rivals. The direct ancestor of the SimCity/Sims itch he clearly has.

> [!failure]- 🚫 Top 5 to Avoid
> 1. [**Krusty's Fun House**](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Krusty%27s%20Fun%20House) `🏷 Licensed Puzzle` — A Lemmings clone riding entirely on Simpsons branding.
> 2. [**Virtual Bart**](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Virtual%20Bart) `🏷 Licensed` — A disjointed minigame collection with no real hook.
> 3. [**The Addams Family**](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/The%20Addams%20Family) `🏷 Licensed Platformer` — Forgettable movie tie-in platforming.
> 4. [**Disney's Timon & Pumbaa's Jungle Games**](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Disney%27s%20Timon%20%26%20Pumbaa%27s%20Jungle%20Games) `🏷 Licensed` — Thin minigame collection.
> 5. [**Ballz 3D**](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Ballz%203D) `🏷 Sports` — A gimmicky pseudo-3D sports/pinball hybrid that didn't age well.
