# 🏆 Platform Power Rankings — Top 10s & Hall of Shame

> [!abstract] What this is
> Top 10 games **per platform**, heavily skewed toward my Backloggd backlog ([ujj](https://www.backloggd.com/u/ujj/)), plus a **Top 5 to AVOID** per platform. Companion doc to [[Backlog Picks]] and [[Backlog Picks - Handheld]].

> [!tip]- 🧬 The Taste Profile (how these were picked)
> Distilled from my ratings & reviews:
> - **❤️ Loved**: NieR Replicant, Drakengard, Death Stranding, Guitar Hero III, Portal, Gotcha Force, Outer Wilds, Tearaway, Gravity Rush, Skate 2/3, Friday Monsters → **auteur vision, narrative gut-punches, rhythm/music games, weird one-off concepts, cozy vibes**.
> - **💔 Bounced off**: Vanquish ("so so bland"), OOT ("the game was working against me" — long retry loops, aimless wandering), Prey (wasted premise), Parasite Eve (survival systems on top of everything), SotC (trial-and-error tedium), Revengeance's Armstrong wall → **allergic to jank, retry loops, grind, blandness, and difficulty walls**.
> - **📝 Key quote**: *"bland combat works wonders… consistent with the emptiness of it all"* (Drakengard) — flaws are fine **when they serve a vision**. Flaws in service of nothing = dropped.

## 🕹️ SNES

> [!tip] 🥇 [[Chrono Trigger]] 📦
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Nintendo%20-%20Super%20Nintendo%20Entertainment%20System/Named_Boxarts/Chrono%20Trigger%20%28USA%29.png) `⏱ ~23h` `🏷 JRPG` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Chrono%20Trigger)
> The anti-grind JRPG: brisk pacing, zero filler, and 12+ endings that reward re-runs the way NieR's routes do. The safest 5★ candidate on the console.

> [!tip] 🥈 [[EarthBound]] 📦
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Nintendo%20-%20Super%20Nintendo%20Entertainment%20System/Named_Boxarts/EarthBound%20%28USA%29.png) `⏱ ~30h` `🏷 JRPG` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/EarthBound)
> Shigesato Itoi's auteur oddball. Quirky, sincere, funny-then-devastating. The direct ancestor of Mother 3, which is already high on my handheld list.

> [!tip] 🥉 [[Live A Live]] 📦 *(newly added!)*
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Nintendo%20-%20Super%20Nintendo%20Entertainment%20System/Named_Boxarts/Live%20A%20Live%20%28Japan%29.png) `⏱ ~16h` `🏷 JRPG · Anthology` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Live%20A%20Live)
> An anthology of 7+ genre-hopping stories that collapse into one dark meta-twist. Structurally the most NieR-like thing on SNES. (Also on Switch as a gorgeous remake — see Switch section; play that version.)

> [!quote] #4 [[Terranigma]] 📦 ⚠️
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Nintendo%20-%20Super%20Nintendo%20Entertainment%20System/Named_Boxarts/Terranigma%20%28Europe%29.png) `⏱ ~28h` `🏷 Action RPG` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Terranigma)
> The melancholy of Drakengard in a 16-bit action-RPG about resurrecting the world. Previously flagged for "dated jank risk" but honestly the action is smooth for its era; the vibe payoff is worth it.

> [!quote] #5 [[Final Fantasy VI]] ✨
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Nintendo%20-%20Super%20Nintendo%20Entertainment%20System/Named_Boxarts/Final%20Fantasy%20III%20%28USA%29.png) `⏱ ~35h` `🏷 JRPG` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Final%20Fantasy%20VI)
> Ensemble-cast tragedy, an opera scene, and a villain who *wins* halfway through. Story-first JRPG design that earns its emotional beats.

> [!quote] #6 [[Super Metroid]] ✨
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Nintendo%20-%20Super%20Nintendo%20Entertainment%20System/Named_Boxarts/Super%20Metroid%20%28USA%2C%20Europe%29%20%28En%2CJa%29%20%28Virtual%20Console%2C%20Classic%20Mini%2C%20Switch%20Online%29.png) `⏱ ~9h` `🏷 Metroidvania` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Super%20Metroid)
> The atmosphere-forward blueprint for Aria of Sorrow (already on my handheld list). Guided enough to avoid the aimless-wandering problem.

> [!quote] #7 [[Yoshi's Island]] ✨
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Nintendo%20-%20Super%20Nintendo%20Entertainment%20System/Named_Boxarts/Super%20Mario%20World%202%20-%20Yoshi%27s%20Island%20%28USA%29.png) `⏱ ~7½h` `🏷 Platformer` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Super%20Mario%20World%202%20Yoshi%27s%20Island)
> Hand-drawn creative excess; every level introduces and discards an idea. Generous checkpointing, low frustration.

> [!quote] #8 [[Kirby Super Star]] ✨
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Nintendo%20-%20Super%20Nintendo%20Entertainment%20System/Named_Boxarts/Kirby%20Super%20Star%20%28USA%29.png) `⏱ ~5½h` `🏷 Platformer · Anthology` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Kirby%20Super%20Star)
> An anthology structure (again!) of mini-campaigns. Pure friction-free joy for between-heavy-games palate cleansing.

> [!quote] #9 [[Super Mario RPG]] ✨
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Nintendo%20-%20Super%20Nintendo%20Entertainment%20System/Named_Boxarts/Super%20Mario%20RPG%20-%20Legend%20of%20the%20Seven%20Stars%20%28USA%2C%20Europe%29%20%28Virtual%20Console%29.png) `⏱ ~17h` `🏷 JRPG` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Super%20Mario%20RPG)
> Short, charming, funny, timing-based combat that stays engaging without ever demanding grind.

> [!quote] #10 [[Super Mario World]] ✨
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Nintendo%20-%20Super%20Nintendo%20Entertainment%20System/Named_Boxarts/Super%20Mario%20World%20%28USA%2C%20Europe%29%20%28Virtual%20Console%2C%20Classic%20Mini%2C%20Switch%20Online%29.png) `⏱ ~6h` `🏷 Platformer` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Super%20Mario%20World)
> The platonic platformer. Snappy retry loop (the exact opposite of OOT's), dense with secrets.


> [!failure]- 🚫 Top 5 to Avoid
> 1. [**Super Ghouls 'n Ghosts**](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Super%20Ghouls%20%27n%20Ghosts) `⏱ ~5h` `🏷 Action Platformer` — Brutal checkpoint-run difficulty *and* it makes you beat the game twice. The Armstrong wall as an entire game.
> 2. [**Secret of Mana**](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Secret%20of%20Mana) `⏱ ~24h` `🏷 Action RPG` — Beloved but held together with tape: broken co-op AI, hit-detection jank, and grinding for weapon/magic levels. Flaws in service of nothing.
> 3. [**Star Fox**](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Star%20Fox) `⏱ ~2h` `🏷 Rail Shooter` — Historically important, plays like a slideshow today. The "I get why it mattered" OOT feeling, minus the highs.
> 4. [**The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past**](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/The%20Legend%20of%20Zelda%3A%20A%20Link%20to%20the%20Past) `⏱ ~15½h` `🏷 Action Adventure` — Genuinely great, but it's cryptic-wandering Zelda with long dungeon retry loops. Given the OOT scars, not the place to reconcile with the series (that's A Link Between Worlds — see 3DS).
> 5. [**Donkey Kong Country**](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Donkey%20Kong%20Country) `⏱ ~4½h` `🏷 Platformer` — Gorgeous, but trial-and-error deaths (memorize-the-barrel design) with stingy checkpoints. Frustration disguised as charm.

## 💿 PlayStation 1

> [!tip] 🥇 [[Klonoa - Door to Phantomile\|Klonoa: Door to Phantomile]] 📦
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Sony%20-%20PlayStation/Named_Boxarts/Klonoa%20-%20Door%20to%20Phantomile%20%28USA%29.png) `⏱ ~5h` `🏷 Platformer` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Klonoa%3A%20Door%20to%20Phantomile)
> Dreamy, short, and hides one of the most quietly devastating endings of the 32-bit era. Tearaway-grade charm with a NieR-grade gut-punch. (The Wii remake in my backlog is the smoother way to play — see Wii.)

> [!tip] 🥈 [[Moon]] 📦
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Sony%20-%20PlayStation/Named_Boxarts/Moon%20-%20Remix%20RPG%20Adventure%20%28Japan%29.png) `⏱ ~13h` `🏷 Anti-RPG · Adventure` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Moon%20Remix%20RPG)
> Love-de-Lic's "anti-RPG": you play the kid who cleans up after the RPG hero. The ancestor of Chibi-Robo and Chulip, pure auteur weirdness. Official English version on Switch if the PS1 route is awkward.

> [!tip] 🥉 [[Silent Hill]] 📦
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Sony%20-%20PlayStation/Named_Boxarts/Silent%20Hill%20%28USA%29.png) `⏱ ~6½h` `🏷 Survival Horror` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Silent%20Hill)
> Gateway to SH3 (newly backlogged). Atmosphere-first horror where the low-poly fog *is* the vision — dated tech in service of something, the Drakengard principle.

> [!quote] #4 [[Final Fantasy VII]] 📦
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Sony%20-%20PlayStation/Named_Boxarts/Final%20Fantasy%20VII%20%28USA%29.png) `⏱ ~37h` `🏷 JRPG` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Final%20Fantasy%20VII)
> Prerequisite for the backlogged Crisis Core, and the series' best blend of weird tonal swings and sincerity. Play with fast-forward on an emulator to defang random-encounter tedium.

> [!quote] #5 [[Vib-Ribbon]] 📦
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Sony%20-%20PlayStation/Named_Boxarts/Vib-Ribbon%20%28Europe%29%20%28En%2CFr%2CDe%2CEs%2CIt%29.png) `⏱ ~2h` `🏷 Rhythm` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Vib-Ribbon)
> A vector-line rhythm game that generates levels from *any* music CD. As a Guitar Hero 5★-er, this is essential heritage. Playable on Vita/PS3 too.

> [!quote] #6 [[Xenogears]] 📦
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Sony%20-%20PlayStation/Named_Boxarts/Xenogears%20%28USA%29.png) `⏱ ~57h` `🏷 JRPG` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Xenogears)
> The most ambitious, messy auteur JRPG on the console. Disc 2 famously collapses into narrated storyboards — but "raw and reckless" is exactly what I praised in Drakengard 3.

> [!quote] #7 [[Koudelka]] 📦 *(newly added)*
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Sony%20-%20PlayStation/Named_Boxarts/Koudelka%20%28USA%29.png) `⏱ ~13h` `🏷 Horror RPG` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Koudelka)
> Gothic horror RPG from ex-Square staff; gorgeous mo-capped cutscenes, genuinely creepy. ⚠️ Combat is slow and clunky — go in for the vibes, not the systems.

> [!quote] #8 [[Wild Arms]] 📦 *(newly added)*
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Sony%20-%20PlayStation/Named_Boxarts/Wild%20Arms%20%28USA%29.png) `⏱ ~28h` `🏷 JRPG` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Wild%20Arms)
> Warm western-flavoured JRPG with an all-timer opening theme. Comfort-food pacing; the Alter Code: F remake in my backlog is an alternative but the original's charm is intact.

> [!quote] #9 [[Final Fantasy VIII]] 📦
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Sony%20-%20PlayStation/Named_Boxarts/Final%20Fantasy%20VIII%20%28USA%29.png) `⏱ ~41h` `🏷 JRPG` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Final%20Fantasy%20VIII)
> Divisive systems, but a dreamlike, melancholic story that swings big. The Remaster's 3x speed + no-encounters toggles remove every reason it used to frustrate.

> [!quote] #10 [[No One Can Stop Mr. Domino!]] 📦
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Sony%20-%20PlayStation/Named_Boxarts/No%20One%20Can%20Stop%20Mr.%20Domino%20%28USA%29.png) `⏱ ~2h` `🏷 Puzzle Action` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/No%20One%20Can%20Stop%20Mr.%20Domino%21)
> A game about being a sentient domino. Twenty-something minutes of concept, zero fat. Exactly the Tokyo Jungle / Mister Mosquito shelf.


> [!failure]- 🚫 Top 5 to Avoid
> 1. [**Tenchu: Stealth Assassins**](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Tenchu%3A%20Stealth%20Assassins) `⏱ ~7h` `🏷 Stealth` 📦 — In my backlog, but it's trial-and-error stealth with brutal checkpoints and a camera from 1998. This is the OOT review waiting to happen.
> 2. [**Breath of Fire IV**](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Breath%20of%20Fire%20IV) `⏱ ~33h` `🏷 JRPG` 📦 — Lovely sprites wrapped around slow, grindy, high-encounter-rate JRPG plumbing. The flaws serve nothing.
> 3. [**Alundra**](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Alundra) `⏱ ~26h` `🏷 Action Adventure · Puzzle` — Praised puzzle-Zelda, but the puzzles are *cruel* and the platforming is pixel-precise. Dungeon-frustration incarnate.
> 4. [**The Legend of Dragoon**](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/The%20Legend%20of%20Dragoon) `⏱ ~45h` `🏷 JRPG` — Timing-based combat sounds appealing, but it gates progress behind repetition (Addition grinding) and a glacial script.
> 5. [**Tomb Raider (1996)**](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Tomb%20Raider%201996) `⏱ ~13h` `🏷 Action Adventure` — Tank controls, save crystals, instant-death leaps. Historically vital, personally miserable.

## 💿 PlayStation 2

> [!tip] 🥇 [[Silent Hill 3]] 📦 *(newly added)*
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Sony%20-%20PlayStation%202/Named_Boxarts/Silent%20Hill%203%20%28USA%29%20%28En%2CJa%2CFr%2CDe%2CEs%2CIt%2CKo%29.png) `⏱ ~6½h` `🏷 Survival Horror` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Silent%20Hill%203)
> One of the best horror games ever made: oppressive, personal, gorgeous. ⏳ Play the backlogged Silent Hill (PS1) first — SH3 is a direct sequel.

> [!tip] 🥈 [[Ōkami]] 📦
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Sony%20-%20PlayStation%202/Named_Boxarts/Ookami%20%28USA%29.png) `⏱ ~30h` `🏷 Action Adventure` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Okami)
> Clover's painterly masterpiece. Long, but generous and gentle; the brush mechanic never stops being satisfying. (Ōkami HD in my backlog is the definitive way — see PS4.)

> [!tip] 🥉 [[Haunting Ground]] 📦
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Sony%20-%20PlayStation%202/Named_Boxarts/Haunting%20Ground%20%28USA%29.png) `⏱ ~9h` `🏷 Survival Horror` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Haunting%20Ground)
> Capcom's most underrated survival horror: you're powerless, your dog Hewie is the mechanic. Rule of Rose's spiritual sibling with far better game-feel.

> [!quote] #4 [[Killer7]] 📦
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Sony%20-%20PlayStation%202/Named_Boxarts/Killer7%20%28USA%29.png) `⏱ ~15h` `🏷 Action Adventure · Rail` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Killer7)
> Suda51's masterpiece and the best possible follow-up to loving Drakengard's "gameplay as vessel" philosophy. On-rails, surreal, unforgettable.

> [!quote] #5 [[Rule of Rose]] 📦 *(newly added)*
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Sony%20-%20PlayStation%202/Named_Boxarts/Rule%20of%20Rose%20%28USA%29.png) `⏱ ~9h` `🏷 Psychological Horror` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Rule%20of%20Rose)
> Psychological horror about childhood cruelty; one of the most disturbing narratives on the console. ⚠️ Combat is genuinely bad — accept it as a vessel (I've done this before).

> [!quote] #6 [[Fatal Frame II - Crimson Butterfly\|Fatal Frame II: Crimson Butterfly]] 📦
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Sony%20-%20PlayStation%202/Named_Boxarts/Fatal%20Frame%20II%20-%20Crimson%20Butterfly%20%28USA%29.png) `⏱ ~9h` `🏷 Survival Horror` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Fatal%20Frame%20II%3A%20Crimson%20Butterfly)
> The scariest game on PS2, and the camera-as-weapon idea is a true one-off. Slow, deliberate dread rather than difficulty walls.

> [!quote] #7 [[Shadow of Destiny]] 📦
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Sony%20-%20PlayStation%202/Named_Boxarts/Shadow%20of%20Destiny%20%28USA%29.png) `⏱ ~7h` `🏷 Adventure · Time Loop` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Shadow%20of%20Destiny)
> A time-looping murder mystery where *you're* the victim. Short, novel, all narrative — a Ghost Trick precursor in spirit.

> [!quote] #8 [[Mister Mosquito]] 📦
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Sony%20-%20PlayStation%202/Named_Boxarts/Mister%20Mosquito%20%28USA%29.png) `⏱ ~4h` `🏷 Action · Oddball` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Mister%20Mosquito)
> You are a mosquito. You bite a Japanese family. That's the game. Peak "only Japan in 2001" energy, and a perfect palate cleanser.

> [!quote] #9 [[Dark Cloud 2]] 📦
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Sony%20-%20PlayStation%202/Named_Boxarts/Dark%20Cloud%202%20%28USA%29%20%28v2.00%29.png) `⏱ ~40h` `🏷 Action RPG · Town-Building` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Dark%20Cloud%202)
> Level-5 comfort-food dungeon crawling with town-building, photography, invention, golf. The Fantasy Life "one more thing" loop, a decade early.

> [!quote] #10 [[God Hand]] 📦 ⚠️
> ![|110](https://thumbnails.libretro.com/Sony%20-%20PlayStation%202/Named_Boxarts/God%20Hand%20%28USA%29.png) `⏱ ~11h` `🏷 Brawler` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/God%20Hand)
> Mikami's swan song at Clover: hilarious, deep brawler. The caution: its dynamic difficulty *will* punch back. Play on easy without shame; drop guilt-free if it turns into Armstrong.


> [!failure]- 🚫 Top 5 to Avoid
> 1. [**Drakengard 2**](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Drakengard%202) `⏱ ~20h` `🏷 Action RPG · Musou` 📦 — In my backlog, but it's the outsourced sequel where the bland combat serves *nothing* — the bleakness is gone, replaced by generic fantasy. Yoko Taro barely touched it. The one Drakengard to skip.
> 2. [**Evergrace**](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Evergrace) `⏱ ~14h` `🏷 Action RPG` 📦 — Early FromSoft jank without the vision that later redeems it. Fascinating soundtrack, miserable everything else.
> 3. [**Baroque**](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Baroque) `⏱ ~14h` `🏷 Roguelike RPG` 📦 — A punishing die-and-lose-everything roguelike wrapped around cryptic storytelling. The survival-layer problem that killed Parasite Eve for me, squared.
> 4. [**Chulip**](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Chulip) `⏱ ~13h` `🏷 Adventure · Oddball` 📦 — Love-de-Lic lineage (like Moon), but *deliberately* obtuse: unexplained schedules, instant fail-states, guide required. Flower, Sun, and Rain-tier frustration risk.
> 5. [**Okage: Shadow King**](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Okage%3A%20Shadow%20King) `⏱ ~20h` `🏷 JRPG` 📦 — Charming shadow-puppet art over some of the slowest, blandest combat on PS2. The Prey pattern: great premise, nothing done with it.

## 💿 PlayStation 3

> [!tip] 🥇 [[Tokyo Jungle]] 📦

> [!tip] 🥉 [[Asura's Wrath]] 📦
> ![|110](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/d/d8/Asura%27s_Wrath_Cover_Art.png) `⏱ ~7½h` `🏷 Interactive Anime · QTE Action` [Backloggd ↗](https://www.backloggd.com/search/games/Asura%27s%20Wrath)
