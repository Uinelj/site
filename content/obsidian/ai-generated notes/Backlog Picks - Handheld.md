# 🕹️ Backlog Picks — Handheld Only

> Based on my Backloggd profile ([ujj](https://www.backloggd.com/u/ujj/)) and recent reviews. Filtered to handheld-native games (DS, 3DS, GBA, PSP, Vita). Pokémon excluded — already playing HeartGold.

---

## The List

### 1. 🔥 Ghost Trick: Phantom Detective
The safest bet on your entire backlog, handheld or not. Inventive puzzles, snappy writing, incredible twists, and zero jank. Originally built *for* DS and it shows — tight, polished, and impossible to put down. If you liked NieR's storytelling, you'll love the reveals here.

### 2. 🎧 The World Ends with You
Unique dual-screen combat, one of the best soundtracks in gaming, and a stylish Shibuya setting dripping with creative identity. Strong narrative about connection and self-worth that actually *earns* its emotional beats. Peak "only on DS" design.

### 3. 🥁 Elite Beat Agents
Guitar Hero III got a perfect 5★ from you. This is DS rhythm perfection — absurd scenarios (saving a baby from a fire by dancing to "Jumpin' Jack Flash"), catchy licensed tracks, and tight gameplay. Pure joy, zero frustration.

### 4. 🔢 Nine Hours, Nine Persons, Nine Doors (999)
A gripping escape-room visual novel with one of the best twist endings in games. If you love NieR's approach to multiple playthroughs revealing the full picture, 999 does something similar — and the DS version's dual screens are *plot-relevant* in a way no other game has pulled off.

### 5. 🌻 Mother 3
One of the most emotionally resonant RPGs ever made. Quirky, funny, heartbreaking — it's the game that makes grown adults cry. Strong auteur vision (Shigesato Itoi), tight pacing, and a rhythm-based combat system that'll click for you. The fan translation is excellent.

### 6. 🦇 Castlevania: Aria of Sorrow
The tightest GBA Metroidvania — excellent game feel, satisfying soul-collecting system, and just the right length (~8 hours). No jank, no bloat, all killer. If you want something you can just *play* without fighting the game, this is it.

### 7. 🕵️ Hotel Dusk: Room 215
A noir-tinged adventure game with a stunning rotoscoped art style unlike anything else on DS. Strong mystery writing, memorable characters, and a great sense of atmosphere. Slow-burn but never aimless — every conversation pulls you deeper in.

### 8. ⏱️ Half-Minute Hero
A hilarious deconstruction of JRPGs where you save the world in 30 seconds, over and over. Creative, funny, zero friction. Each mode reinvents itself. Perfect portable game — short bursts, constant novelty.

### 9. 🐕 Solatorobo: Red the Hunter
A charming action-RPG set in a gorgeous floating-islands world of anthropomorphic dogs and cats. Beautiful sprite+3D art, fun mech-riding combat, and a surprisingly deep story that unfolds in the second half. CyberConnect2 at their most heartfelt.

### 10. 🏡 Fantasy Life
Cozy Level-5 RPG where you pick a "Life" (paladin, blacksmith, cook, angler…) and just vibe. Tons of variety, charming writing, and the kind of game where you look up and 40 hours have vanished. Great for when you want something relaxing between heavier picks.

## ⚠️ Avoid for now

- **The Legend of Zelda: Phantom Hourglass** — The repeated Temple of the Ocean King is one of the most frustrating loops in Zelda. Given how OOT's retry loops burned you, this will be *worse*. Save for a very patient day.
- **The Rub Rabbits!** — Sequel to Project Rub, which you gave 2★. Same formula, same issues.
- **Children of Mana** — Extremely repetitive dungeon crawler. Looks pretty, plays like a chore. Would annoy you fast.
- **Flower, Sun, and Rain** — Suda51 game but *deliberately* obtuse and janky. The puzzles require a guidebook (literally). Even Suda fans are split. High frustration risk.
- **Crisis Core: Final Fantasy VII** — You haven't played FF VII yet (it's in your backlog). Play the original first.
- **Contact** — Interesting concept but sluggish execution and lots of grinding. The meta-narrative is clever but the gameplay doesn't hold up.
- **Boku no Natsuyasumi Portable** — Requires Japanese proficiency to enjoy fully. Very low "game" content — mostly vibes. Could feel like a lesser Friday Monsters situation.

## ⏳ Play first entries first
- **Pokémon Black / Black 2** — Finish HeartGold first, then these are waiting for you. Gen V has the best story in the series.
- **Valkyrie Profile: Lenneth** — Great but very systems-heavy and can be punishing. Not an "avoid" — just save it for when you want something crunchy.
- **Tactics Ogre: Let Us Cling Together** — Same as above. Excellent but demanding. Approach when in a strategic mood.
- **Linda Cube Again** — Fascinating but very niche. Japanese-only and systems-heavy. For the adventurous mood.
